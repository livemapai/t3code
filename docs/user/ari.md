# Ari

Settings → Ari holds the switches for the Ari features (merge shout, thread flags, the Relay section, team threads, Ari Library links) and shows the Ari CLI account and relay health on each connected server host. On mobile, open the environment in Settings to see its Ari agent host panel. Fix commands run on the named server, including when you connect remotely. Reading this information requires the `ari:read` permission; guest thread access does not include it.

A quiet sidebar dot appears when relay delivery needs attention or messages are held. Held messages on your current environment open the Ari relay site; other environments open their settings. Unbound held messages appear only in the hub’s account-wide list until Ari binds them to an environment. Approval and trust decisions stay on the Ari site. Held messages show senders only.

Threads containing an Ari relay marker show the sender in a badge. In the web and desktop apps, a thread that a relay message started carries the `relay` tag and a relay icon in the sidebar, and sits in a Relay section there, apart from the threads you started; threads started before this was added do not. A relay thread that waits for your approval or answer, or that failed, shows with your own threads until that is dealt with. One you write in yourself stays with your own threads, and one you pin, snooze or settle goes where those go. Settings → Ari → Relay section turns the section off; the icon stays. Folders have no Relay section. The marker is message text and can be forged; the badge, the icon and the section do not verify identity or grant permissions.

## Team threads

Your threads are private. You can show one to your team so teammates know what you are working on: right-click it in the sidebar, or open its menu from the thread title, and choose Show to team. Choose Hide from team to take it off the list; that takes effect at once. A thread you show carries a people icon in the sidebar and a Team badge next to its title. Nothing is shown unless you choose it, and there is no way to show all your threads at once.

For a shown thread, the team sees only its title, project, branch, pull request link, whether its agent is working or idle, and when that last changed. They never see messages, and an agent waiting for your approval or answer, or one that stopped with an error, reads as idle. Archiving or deleting a thread hides it too. Your threads are listed through the Ari account signed in on the machine that runs them, and drop off the list about 15 minutes after that machine goes offline or its T3 Code stops.

In the web and desktop apps, the Team section at the bottom of the sidebar lists the threads your teammates show, grouped by person. Click one to ask that teammate's agent about it (a new thread opens with the question started in the composer; finish it and send it yourself), open its pull request, or copy its title. The section stays hidden when nobody shows anything or Ari on the server host is missing, signed out, or too old for team threads; `ari update` adds support. Settings → Ari → Team threads turns the section and the Show to team action off. It does not hide threads you already show; hide those from their menu.
